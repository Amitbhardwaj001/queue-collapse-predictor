AI Queue Collapse Predictor

Predicting overcrowded queues before they happen — with computer vision and machine learning

Team: ____________________  |  Guide: ____________________  |  Department: ____________________

The problem

Canteens, hospitals, banks and ticket counters only react after a queue has already become too long. By the time staff notice, people are waiting 15–20 minutes, some give up and leave, and opening another counter takes time.

CCTV cameras can show how long the queue is now, but not how long it will be.

Our solution

A system that watches the queue through a camera, counts people automatically, learns how the queue grows, and predicts the queue size, waiting time and risk of collapse 10–15 minutes ahead.

It moves queue management from reactive to proactive: it recommends opening a counter before congestion happens.

What the model looks at

  • Current queue length
  • Queue 1, 2, 3, 5 and 10 minutes ago
  • How fast the queue is growing or shrinking, and how unsteady it is
  • Time of day (rush hours) and day of the week
  • Number of open counters and service speed (people served per minute)

Waiting time = people in queue ÷ (counters × people served per counter per minute)
