AI Queue Collapse Predictor

One engine that predicts when a system will collapse under demand — and how many servers prevent it

Team: ____________________  |  Guide: ____________________  |  Department: ____________________

The problem

Canteens, hospitals, web servers and call centres all fail the same way: demand briefly exceeds capacity, a backlog builds, waiting explodes and people give up. Managers notice only after it has happened.

Monitoring shows the backlog now. Nobody tells them when it will collapse or how many servers prevent it.

Our solution

A general collapse-prediction platform. Anything with arrivals, a service capacity and a backlog plugs in as a domain. For each one the system forecasts the backlog, runs the Collapse Radar and answers three questions:

  • When will it collapse? (time-to-collapse with a range)
  • How likely is it? (probability from 500 simulated futures)
  • What should we do? (what-if staffing simulator)

How it works

1. SenseCamera + YOLOv8 people counting, or a metric feed (queue depth)
→
2. HistoryBacklog stored per minute
→
3. XGBoostForecasts the backlog and critical-wait probability
→
4. Collapse RadarMonte Carlo queue simulation of 500 futures per staffing option
→
5. DecisionTime-to-collapse, risk, safe staffing, advice

What the model looks at

  • Current backlog and the backlog 1, 2, 3, 5 and 10 minutes ago
  • Growth, volatility, time of day and weekday
  • Open servers and service speed (capacity)

Wait = backlog ÷ (servers × items served per server per minute)

Collapse Radar

  • Turns the forecast into an implied arrival rate per minute
  • Replays it with random arrivals and service for every staffing option
  • A path collapses when the wait reaches the critical level
  • Reads off probability, time to collapse, uncertainty band and the smallest safe number of servers
